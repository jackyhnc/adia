# Adia — Privacy Policy

**Effective: 2026-05-18**

We collect as little as we can. Here's exactly what.

## What stays on your device

- **Your screenshots.** Captured at ~1 frame per 1.5s during an active session. Sent to Anthropic for on-task classification, never stored, never sent to us.
- **Your task description and success criteria.** Sent to Anthropic with each screenshot. Never sent to us.
- **Your Anthropic API key.** Stored in macOS Keychain. Never leaves your machine.
- **Session history.** Stored in `UserDefaults` for crash recovery only. Not sent anywhere.
- **Conversation transcripts** (when you chat with Adia). Sent to Anthropic for response. Not stored by us.

## What we collect

- **License email and Stripe customer ID.** For billing.
- **License key activation events.** Machine fingerprint (CPU + macOS major version, hashed), activation timestamp. For piracy prevention and seat counting.
- **Crash reports** (opt-in). No screen content, no task content. macOS version, app version, stack trace only.
- **Anonymous usage events** (opt-in): app launched, session started, session ended (with verified/not-verified bit). No content.

## What we never collect

- Screen content.
- Task descriptions or success criteria.
- Chat transcripts.
- Anthropic API responses.
- Names of websites you visit.
- Anything else from your screen.

## Third parties

- **Anthropic** (`api.anthropic.com`) — processes your screenshots and chat messages under their own privacy policy.
- **Stripe** — processes payments. They see your email, billing address, and card info. We see only the customer ID.
- **Vercel / Cloudflare** — hosts our website and license API. They log IP addresses per their privacy policies.
- **Resend** — sends your license email.

We do not use Google Analytics, Meta pixels, or any ad tracking.

## Data retention

- License records: kept for the life of your account + 7 years for tax/legal.
- Crash reports / usage events: 90 days, then deleted.
- Stripe data: per Stripe's retention policy.

## Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

- **Access**: email `privacy@adia.app` for a copy of what we have.
- **Delete**: email `privacy@adia.app`. We delete within 30 days (except where retention is legally required).
- **Export**: same address.

## Children

Adia is not intended for users under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

Material changes will be announced via email to license holders. The "Effective" date at the top will change.
