# Adia — product & pricing

## Positioning

> The friend in your notch who keeps you on task.

Adia lives in your MacBook notch. You tell it what you're working on. It watches your screen, calls you out when you wander, blocks distracting sites, and verifies you actually finished. Designed for students, indie hackers, and anyone with ADHD or a deadline.

## Target users

- **Students** — essays, problem sets, applications
- **Founders / indie hackers** — deep work blocks
- **Knowledge workers** — proposal writing, code review, design

## Pricing

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| **Trial** | Free for 7 days | Full Adia Pro, no card required |
| **Adia Pro (monthly)** | $7 / month | Everything |
| **Adia Pro (yearly)** | $59 / year ($4.92/mo, save 30%) | Everything + 2 months free |
| **Lifetime** | $149 one-time (early bird, first 500) | Forever, all future updates |

After early bird: lifetime jumps to $249.

Students get **50% off** with a `.edu` email (manual code on request).

## Why this model

- **Bring-your-own OpenAI key** — Adia never pays for inference, so margins are clean and we don't gate features behind usage tiers.
- **$7/mo is impulse territory** — same range as Cron, Raycast Pro, Things 3 subscription.
- **Lifetime tier** captures power users early and seeds the launch with cash.
- **No free forever tier** — focus app for committed users; the 7-day trial is generous enough.

## Acquisition channels (v1)

1. **Product Hunt launch** — Tuesday, week 2 after soft launch.
2. **Twitter/X** — focus/productivity community, indie hackers.
3. **Hacker News Show HN** — angle: "macOS notch-native focus app powered by OpenAI vision."
4. **Reddit** — r/macapps, r/productivity, r/getdisciplined.
5. **TikTok / Instagram Reels** — short demo of the call-out moment.

## Metrics that matter

- Trial → paid conversion (target: 8%)
- Day-7 active during trial (target: 60%)
- Monthly churn (target: <5%)
- LTV / CAC
